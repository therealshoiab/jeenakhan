# JEENA KHAN — High Fashion Model Portfolio

Official luxury editorial and high-fashion portfolio website for **Jeena Khan** ([@j__monra](https://instagram.com/j__monra)), international model hailing from Kashmir, India.

## 🌐 Live Portfolio Website
👉 **[https://therealshoiab.github.io/jeenakhan/](https://therealshoiab.github.io/jeenakhan/)**

---

## ✨ Features
- **Editorial Cover & Vitals**: Unobstructed high-fashion hero cover with quick vitals strip and dual floating look & motion cards.
- **Floating Capsule Header**: Frosted glassmorphic pill navigation with quick Instagram link, Bookings pill, and Dark / Kashmiri Royal Bright theme toggle.
- **Categorized Editorial Gallery**: 89 looks categorized across Editorial, High Fashion, Commercial & Beauty, and Polaroids & Digitals, with smooth horizontal category scroll and fullscreen Lightbox.
- **Audio-Enabled Motion Reels**: 12 runway motion clips with synchronized sound controls.
- **Model Vitals & Measurements**: Imperial & Metric conversion toggle with one-click spec sheet copy.
- **Agency Composite Card**: Standard 4-look uncropped agency composite card with one-click print/save functionality.
- **Dual Aesthetic Themes**:
  - **Luxury Dark Mode**: Onyx, obsidian, and brushed gold.
  - **Kashmiri Royal Bright Mode**: Warm ivory, ruby crimson, and antique heirloom gold.
- **Mobile First**: 100% responsive layout with zero horizontal overflow and fast WebP thumbnails.
